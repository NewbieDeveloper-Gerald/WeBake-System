Our Story

Crumbs N' Roll Bakery

Crumbs N' Roll Bakery (CRB) is a trusted local bakery established in 2010 by Angelita B. Amadeo. Located at 1356 Cordero Street, Lambakin, Marilao, Bulacan , our bakery was founded with a heartfelt purpose: to provide quality, freshly baked, and affordable bread products while creating a sustainable livelihood for our family and community.

Over the years, CRB Bakery has grown into a beloved neighborhood bakery and supplier for cafes, local stores, and bread lovers, while continuing to warmly serve walk-in customers whenever fresh batches emerge from our ovens.

Our operations are powered by a dedicated team of eight skilled bakers and personnel handling daily baking, hygienic packaging, and prompt deliveries across key areas.
