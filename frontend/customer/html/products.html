50% Downpayment Policy

Crumbs N' Roll Bakery — Wholesale Terms

Please read and understand our downpayment and order terms before proceeding to pay:

1. 50% Advance Commitment

WeBake exclusively accepts wholesale bulk orders (minimum 300 bundles). A mandatory 50% advance downpayment via GCash or Maya is required to reserve your baking batch and prevent fraudulent or bogus bookings.

2. Remaining 50% Balance

The remaining 50% balance is strictly due and collected upon order turnover — either upon delivery by our bakery vehicle or at our agreed halfway meeting point.

3. Admin Payment Verification

After you submit your payment reference number and screenshot receipt, our administrator manually verifies the transaction against official GCash / Maya merchant records before approving the order.

4. Cancellation & Refund Terms

You may cancel your order and request a full refund of your 50% downpayment only while the status is "Under Verification". Once the bakery approves the payment and moves the order to "In Production", cancellations and refunds are strictly no longer permitted because raw ingredients and customized baking batches have already been committed.
